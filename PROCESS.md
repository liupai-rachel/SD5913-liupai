How I Used AI

I used ChatGPT to help me brainstorm ideas, organise the essay, improve my English, and find relevant references.

One idea I kept was the difference between ‘asking a machine to make something and understanding what you are asking it to make’. This helped me explain why I still want to learn programming even if AI can generate code.

I also kept the idea of programming as another material for digital design, because it connects with my background in product design.

What I Rejected

I rejected the idea that designers should learn programming mainly because it would make them more competitive or because technology is becoming more important.

Although this sounded reasonable, it was too general and could apply to almost any design student. It did not explain why I personally want to learn programming.

I changed the argument to focus on understanding and controlling AI-generated work, and connected it to my own project, ‘Echoes of the City’.

I also rewrote some AI-generated sentences that sounded too formal or generic because I wanted the essay to sound more like my own writing.
